I've installed a trusted aide and friend on my throne. He is strong enough and loyal enough to bear the weight of the throne and those who dream against it without losing his sanity. For now I must escape the nightmare realm. With my power withering away as it is I can only afford but a precious few decades outside of my domain before I die. In that time I must find a remedy... and a fool to test it on.
